Everfield

Worlds that remember

Everfield Interactive makes deep action RPGs, set in worlds that remember what you did.

We build games where choices outlast the moment. Characters grow from what they have actually done. The people you meet keep their own lives, and their own grudges. The land keeps a record of what happened to it.

Art, sound and systems are designed together from the first sketch, and everything is made to reward a closer look.

Cael: Everfield

A dark fantasy action RPG, complete on your own and built for company.

In development

Four stones mark the road through the design. Walk on to read them.

Follow development

  1. Mortality has weight

    Death comes in stages: a first, a second and a third, and then the Otherworld. In Hardcore, the last death is final.

  2. Build what you’ve done

    Your character grows from what you actually do. A small share of growth comes with each level. The rest is yours to place, and your deeds earn more.

  3. A world that remembers, not one that waits

    The world moves on without you. People you wrong can carry a grudge for life, and a creature hunted to its last is gone for good.

  4. Solo-complete, co-op-native

    Every part of the game can be finished alone. Up to six players can share a world, and the game is built for them from the start.
